Proof & Lab

Early proof, stated honestly.

Opsenta is pre-revenue and in development. No invented customers or numbers — here is what has actually been built and tested.

Motor-vibration MVP

Watches electric-motor vibration: CSV in, baseline learning, sustained-change alerts and a dashboard.

NASA bearing dataset

Tested on the public NASA bearing dataset, where it flagged a real bearing about 55 hours before failure.

Simulated plant, live

A simulated tank / pump / heater plant now runs live with Modbus data, a PLC program (OpenPLC), SCADA (FUXA / Node-RED) and a DWSIM starter model.

What this does — and does not — claim

  • Does: show sustained-drift detection and plain-language alerting can work on real public data and a live simulated plant.
  • Does not: claim deployed customer results, savings figures, or production readiness. Status: in development / early access.
