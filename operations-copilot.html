Operations Copilot

Why is a unit drifting — and what should we do? Opsenta answers in plain language, grounded in your plant’s data and context.

Join early access Emissions Copilot →

What it does

Reads what operators read

Historian data, alarms, P&IDs and shift logs — together, not in silos — to explain performance and surface likely causes.

Yield & energy optimisation

Recommendations aimed at protecting yield and reducing energy use, shown with operational constraints in mind.

Live equipment watch

Learns a machine’s normal behaviour, alerts on sustained drift, ignores single spikes — so teams act early, not late.

Human in command

Every suggestion is advisory. Operators and engineers validate and decide. Opsenta never controls the plant.

How it works, in order

Data in → drift detection → plain-language cause → output-checked action → human decides. The full flow is on the Platform page.

Early MVP today

An early MVP watches electric-motor vibration: CSV in, baseline learning, sustained-change alerts and a dashboard. See Proof & Lab for honest results, including NASA bearing data.

Next: cut emissions, not output

When drift is understood, the Emissions Copilot finds compliant actions that protect throughput, yield and output.
