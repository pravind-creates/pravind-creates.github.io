Platform

One shared plant-data layer. Two copilots on top.

Opsenta works with your existing plant systems — no rip-and-replace — and turns live data and plant context into explanations operators can act on.

Join early access Operations Copilot →

How it works

1 · Data in

Plant overview reading live data over standard protocols including Modbus and OPC UA, plus historian data, alarms, P&IDs and shift logs.

2 · Drift detection

Live equipment watch learns a machine’s normal behaviour, alerts on sustained drift and ignores single spikes.

3 · Plain-language cause

Drift alarms with a likely cause in plain words, so engineers can validate fast.

4 · Output-checked action

Emissions actions ranked with output and margin shown side by side, checked against constraints and product specs.

5 · Human decides

Advisory only — Opsenta recommends, humans decide, it never controls the plant.

Platform FAQ

Does Opsenta control the plant?

No. It is advisory only — it recommends, humans decide, it never controls the plant.

Do we need to replace systems?

No. Opsenta is designed to work with existing plant systems and standard protocols.

Who is it for?

B2B only: refineries, petrochemical and process plants. Beachhead in India, then Middle East, then worldwide.
