About

Operations sentinel, a watchful copilot.

Opsenta — short for operations sentinel — was founded to give plant teams a watchful copilot that explains drift and finds emissions cuts that do not cost output.

Founder: Pravind Thakur

Pravind Thakur is a solo founder with a chemical engineering background and experience as a digitalisation consultant in oil & gas. He is building Opsenta for refineries, petrochemical and process plants — starting in India, then the Middle East and worldwide.

How we work

B2B only. Advisory only — humans decide. Output-neutral emissions actions. Explainable in plain words. Works with existing systems, no rip-and-replace.

Team size: solo founder (0–1). Stage: pre-revenue, in development, early access.
