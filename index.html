The problem we solve

Drift is hard to explain

Operators see alarms and trends, but the cause is buried across historians, logs and drawings.

Emissions vs. output fear

Plants fear cutting emissions means cutting production — so good actions stall.

Compliance is painful

Methane and carbon reporting under EU, US, OGMP and India rules is manual and fragmented.

Built on principles, like a good operator

Output-neutral by design

Every emissions recommendation is checked against operating constraints and product specs before it is shown.

Explainable, in plain words

Drift alarms come with a likely cause people can validate — not a black-box score.
