How it works

1 · Data in

Live plant data over Modbus / OPC UA, plus historian data, alarms, P&IDs and shift logs.

2 · Drift detection

Live equipment watch learns normal behaviour and alerts on sustained drift, not single spikes.

3 · Plain-language cause

Each alert comes with a likely cause operators and engineers can validate quickly.

4 · Output-checked action

Suggested actions are checked against operating constraints and product specs, with emissions next to output / margin impact.

5 · Human decides

Advisory only. Opsenta recommends; people decide. It never controls the plant.

FAQ

Does Opsenta control the plant?

No. It is advisory only — it recommends, humans decide, it never controls the plant.

Will it cut output to cut emissions?

No, by rule. Every suggested emissions action must not reduce throughput, yield or output, and is shown with output / margin impact.

Do we need to replace our systems?

No. Opsenta works with existing plant systems over standard protocols such as Modbus and OPC UA.

Who is it for?

Refineries, petrochemical and process plants (B2B only), starting in India under CCTS, then the Middle East and worldwide.

What stage is Opsenta at, and what does it cost?

Pre-revenue, in development, early access. Pricing is not public or final, so no prices are listed here.
